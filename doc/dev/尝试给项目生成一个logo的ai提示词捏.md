## 🎯 项目核心要素（给 AI 的上下文）

| 要素 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **项目名** | `qwq-flytre-bingo-booster` — `qwq` 是一个颜文字（看起来像在哭的猫猫脸 😿），体现了项目的可爱/搞怪风格 |

## 🅰️ 像素风 — Minecraft 风格

> 适合 GitHub 仓库封面 / Spigot 资源页横幅

```
A Minecraft pixel-art logo for a server plugin called "qwq-flytre-bingo-booster".
The center shows a Bingo card with 4 marked squares in team colors: red (#FF4444),
yellow (#FFAA00), green (#44FF44), and blue (#4444FF).
Above the card floats a Minecraft grass block.
Below it, the text "qwq" rendered in a cute pixel font where the two q's form
a crying anime face (qwq).
Background: dark stone brick pattern, isometric pixel art style, 512x512 resolution.
```

> 适合 Discord 服务器头像 / 游戏内图标

```
Modern flat vector logo. A white Bingo ball with the number "7" in the center,
surrounded by 4 overlapping colored rings — red, yellow, green, blue — each
representing a team. A tiny "qwq" face is subtly embossed in the center of the ball.
Clean minimal lines, no text. Transparent background, rounded square crop.

at small sizes (64x64).
```

## 🅲 萌系颜文字风 — qwq 吉祥物

> 适合社区贴纸 / QQ 群头像 / 社交平台

```
Cute anime mascot character logo. The mascot is a small ghost-like creature whose
face is the "qwq" expression — the two q's are its closed crying eyes, and the w
is its mouth. It wears a Minecraft player skin tunic. It holds a wooden Bingo board
in its tiny hands. Pastel color palette with red/yellow/green/blue accents on the
board. Soft shading, sticker art style, transparent background, 1024x1024.
Standing alone with no text.
```

## 📝 通用原则

- Minecraft 像素字体可用 [minecraft.ttf](https://www.dafont.com/minecraft.font)
- 所有设计最好保留透明背景（`png`）和方形裁剪（`1:1`），方便适配 GitHub 仓库头像、Spigot 资源图标、Discord 服务器图标等不同场景
